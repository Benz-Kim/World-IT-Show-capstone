# Research method
## Research question
When does an exhibition demonstration become a defensible operational value proposition?

The unit of analysis is a specific claim, not a company's overall reputation. Every case connects the proposed mechanism to a deployment constraint and a way to disprove the value proposition.

## Evidence ladder
1. Team artifact: records what was presented, without independently proving it.
2. Vendor or partner description: supports attribution of a product claim.
3. Independent technical or accounting reference: explains a mechanism or measurement boundary.
4. Observed result: requires an identified dataset, protocol and result record.

This repository currently reaches levels 1–3. No field performance results, interviews reconstructed from memory, customer revenue or measured savings are represented as verified.

## Claim record
Use: ID; claim; source ID and locator; evidence class; temporal scope; contradiction; proposed test; disposition. Dispositions are supported-as-attributed, unresolved, contradicted or excluded. A vendor statement can be supported-as-attributed while its operational effectiveness remains unresolved.

## Comparative method
Compare use cases through reliability, integration effort, human workflow, cost boundary and evidence quality. Do not add these into a synthetic investment score: absent deployment data would make the numerical ranking arbitrary.

## Time and authorship
Historical decks are team artifacts. The 2026 synthesis is a later analytical layer. Source-file metadata does not prove event attendance or presentation date. Shared names do not establish who wrote each slide. No assignment of exclusive authorship is made.

## Repository design references
[Stanford CRFM HELM](https://github.com/stanford-crfm/helm) motivates explicit evaluation dimensions and limitations; this project does not claim to reproduce its evaluations. [Awesome Public Datasets](https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets) motivates a navigable source register. The case-study format and claim ledger here are original adaptations, not copied code or an institutional endorsement.

## Updating a case
Add the source and access date first; update claim disposition next; revise the case conclusion only when the evidence changes. Keep unresolved contradictions visible. Update the root navigation in the same work cycle.
