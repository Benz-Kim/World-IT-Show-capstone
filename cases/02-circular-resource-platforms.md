# Circular resources: records versus physical outcomes
## Decision
A digital record can make a waste workflow easier to inspect; recycling and climate benefit still require physical and accounting evidence.

W01 presents ECOYA as a business resource-circulation platform. V03 describes an ECOYA Earth consumer collection and rewards interface. The entities and product scope must be reconciled before these are treated as one feature set. The partner portfolio supports the interface description, not audited recycling outcomes.

## Causal chain
Collection request → pickup → weighing → sorting → processor receipt → usable recovered material → actual substitution.

Each arrow can lose material or break custody. A GPS record supports location, not composition. An immutable transaction cannot fix a false weight at entry. A dashboard can increase visibility without increasing recovery.

## Measurement design — proposed
Define a batch identifier and reconcile input mass with recovered output, rejects, moisture change and inventory change. Record scale calibration, material type, contamination and the receiving processor. Prevent duplicated batch IDs across downstream reports.

A recovery ratio should state its denominator: usable recovered material divided by collected mass is different from collected mass divided by material generated. Report both only when each denominator is measured.

## Climate boundary
T02 distinguishes waste-treatment accounting from additional recycling metrics. State organizational boundary, treatment route, emission-factor source and reporting period. Avoided virgin production belongs in an explicitly separated counterfactual calculation; it should not be casually subtracted from inventory emissions.

## Commercial diligence
| Possible value | Needed evidence | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower administrative cost | Before/after reconciliation time with comparable workload | Digitizing duplicate manual entry |
| Higher recovered value | Net proceeds after pickup, sorting and contamination charges | Gross sale price masks logistics loss |
| More reliable reporting | Batch-level audit trail and downstream receipts | Attractive totals lack custody evidence |

No measured recycling yield, carbon reduction, customer return or independent assurance is available in the supplied materials. Conclusions therefore concern measurement design rather than environmental performance.
