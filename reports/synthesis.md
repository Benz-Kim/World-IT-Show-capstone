# From exhibition claims to operational evidence
## Finding
Across patrol robotics, circular-resource platforms and immersive environments, the limiting question is not whether a demonstration is technically plausible. It is whether the complete workflow produces a measurable result under the buyer's constraints.

This is a comparative interpretation of the supplied artifacts, not a measured cross-company ranking.

| Case | Attractive mechanism | Missing bridge | Practical decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patrol robot | Mobile sensing and remote intervention | Site reliability and operator workload | Resolve configuration, then supervised trial |
| Resource platform | Traceable collection and reporting | Physical custody and consistent accounting | Audit a batch before accepting impact claims |
| Immersive system | Interactive representation | Task validation and data/model fidelity | Define target user outcome before procurement |

## Three conclusions
First, attribution is a result. Discovering that a number came from a presentation rather than an independent trial changes how confidently it can be used.

Second, integration can dominate component capability. A camera specification, ledger feature or rendered scene says little about the weakest handoff in the operating chain.

Third, commercial value needs a buyer-specific denominator. Useful patrol hours, recovered usable mass and successful user tasks are more informative than broad sector growth forecasts.

## Scope
The activity record describes WIS participation in 2023–25. Available source artifacts directly cover 2023 and 2025 topics; a 2024 reconstruction is not invented. The source register documents access and attribution limitations. No interviews are quoted without records.

## Research progression
The source presentations supplied the initial product narratives. This repository adds a claim ledger, contradictions, technical context and explicit evaluation designs in October 2026. Proposed experiments remain proposals until dated result records are added.
